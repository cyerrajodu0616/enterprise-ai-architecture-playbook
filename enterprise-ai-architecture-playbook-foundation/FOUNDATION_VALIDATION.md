## Quality Gates

### Accuracy — Pass

The foundation focuses on architectural principles and methods rather than time-sensitive vendor claims. It avoids unsupported universal recommendations.

### Completeness — Pass for Foundation v1

The pack includes:

- repository purpose and audience;
- transition from engineering decisions to architectural decisions;
- Why-before-How principle;
- Simplicity Test;
- explicit assumptions;
- alternatives and counterarguments;
- cost and operations;
- security and governance;
- evidence and evaluation;
- lesson structure;
- ADR standard;
- review workflow;
- definition of done;
- eight-week curriculum;
- HealthSure evolution;
- weekly exit criteria;
- final capability questions.

### Clarity — Pass

The documents are written for experienced engineers moving toward architecture responsibilities. Requirements, constraints, and assumptions are explicitly separated.

### Practicality — Pass

The guidelines can be reused directly as the governing prompt and review checklist for every future lesson.

### Simplicity — Pass

The Simplicity Test appears in the README, manifesto, guidelines, and roadmap.

### Counterarguments — Pass

Every recommendation must include the strongest reasonable counterargument. Each roadmap week includes counterarguments to investigate.

### Cost and Operations — Pass

Cost and operations are first-class design concerns rather than afterthoughts.

### Security and Governance — Pass

Identity, authorization, sensitive-data handling, auditability, privacy, and governance are included in the lesson standard and roadmap.

### Timelessness — Pass

The README and manifesto avoid dependence on current products. The roadmap places technology inside decision contexts.

### Adaptability — Pass

Every decision must state assumptions and what would change the recommendation. Weekly review requires revisiting ADRs.
