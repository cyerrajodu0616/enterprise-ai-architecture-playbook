# Enterprise AI Architecture Playbook Guidelines

## Standard Lesson Structure

1. Executive Summary
2. Business Problem (HealthSure)
3. Naive Explanation
4. Technical Deep Dive
5. Architecture Perspective
6. Alternatives
7. Counterarguments
8. Cost Analysis
9. Operational Concerns
10. Security & Governance
11. Future Evolution
12. Recommendation

## Required Outputs

- HTML page
- ADR
- Interview Cheat Sheet
- HealthSure Update
- Architect's Challenge
- Open Questions
- Architect's Reflection

Repository Motto:

> Architectural decisions are temporary. Architectural reasoning is timeless.
