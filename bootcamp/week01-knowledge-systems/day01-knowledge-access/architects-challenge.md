# Architect's Challenge — Day 1

HealthSure proposes one centralized AI knowledge platform containing policy, claims, benefit accumulators, and provider/network data.

Prepare a recommendation that covers:
- business problem;
- assumptions;
- simplest viable solution;
- alternatives;
- strongest counterargument;
- cost;
- operations;
- security/governance;
- change triggers.

Start with:
> Which facts are needed, which source is authoritative, and what freshness/correctness/latency requirements apply?
