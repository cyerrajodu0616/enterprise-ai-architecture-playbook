# Architect's Challenge — Day 3

HealthSure discovers that an approved amendment has unchanged content but corrected applicability metadata extending it into earlier historical months. Historical answers were generated under the previous metadata. At the same time, a newly effective policy package remains incomplete because one required attachment is under review. The authoritative source has unresolved historical-retention, retrieval, and authorization constraints.

The new effective date is approaching or may already have passed. Do not assume the old complete package remains applicable, that the corrected amendment can activate automatically, or that all historical answers require rerunning.

Prepare a recommendation that states:

- known facts;
- assumptions;
- open questions;
- recommendation;
- credible alternatives;
- strongest counterargument;
- smallest defensible impact-analysis scope;
- cost implications;
- operational ownership, replay, remediation, fallback, and review capacity;
- security, authorization, retention, and governance boundaries;
- failure and abstention behavior;
- evidence required before activation or remediation;
- concrete change triggers.

Address both content lineage and resolver lineage. Explain whether originals must be retained, how incomplete packages behave at the effective-date boundary, and how protected historical request state can be reconstructed without retaining unnecessary sensitive data.

There is no single correct final answer. Do not turn this hypothetical scenario into a HealthSure fact.
