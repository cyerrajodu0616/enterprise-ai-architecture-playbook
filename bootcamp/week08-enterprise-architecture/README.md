# Week 8 — Integrated Enterprise Architecture

**Status:** Not Started

## Architectural Capability

Integrate prior decisions into a coherent architecture and adapt the reasoning to unfamiliar businesses.

## Why This Week Exists

Architecture creates value when separate decisions form an explainable investment sequence with explicit risks, ownership, and change triggers.

## HealthSure Business Trigger

The complete architecture must support executive approval, security review, operational readiness, and phased investment.

## Major Topics

- Architecture synthesis, capability mapping, migration, runway, executive communication, sequencing, technical debt, portfolio review, and cross-domain adaptation.

## Decisions to Make

- Which HealthSure decisions remain valid, should change, or can be removed?
- What should be built now, deferred, purchased, or rejected?
- How should the architecture, risks, cost, and migration path be communicated?

## Counterarguments to Explore

- A complete target state may encourage premature investment.
- Standardization may obscure domain-specific constraints.
- Portfolio polish can hide weak evidence or unresolved operating risk.

## Expected Artifacts

- Final HealthSure design and narrative, executive memo, ADR index, risk register, cost model, migration roadmap, and portfolio plan.

## Exit Criteria

Clarify an unfamiliar business problem, recommend the simplest phased architecture, defend its trade-offs, and state the evidence and triggers that would change it.
