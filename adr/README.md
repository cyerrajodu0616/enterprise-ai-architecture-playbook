# Architecture Decision Records

An Architecture Decision Record (ADR) captures why a consequential decision was made, the assumptions and constraints under which it is valid, credible alternatives, the strongest counterargument, trade-offs, cost, operational and governance implications, and review triggers.

## Naming

Use `ADR-0001-decision-name.md`, with a zero-padded sequence and concise lowercase slug.

## Statuses

- **Proposed** — under review; not yet accepted.
- **Accepted** — approved under its documented assumptions.
- **Superseded** — replaced by a later ADR, which must be linked.
- **Deprecated** — retained for history but no longer recommended.
- **Rejected** — considered and intentionally not adopted.

Every ADR should link to the lesson that produced it and any affected HealthSure decision or architecture version. Add it to the [ADR index](index.md) when created.
