# HealthSure Business Context

## Enterprise Profile

HealthSure is a fictional regulated insurance enterprise. It administers insurance products and supports policy servicing, claims, underwriting, provider interactions, compliance, and customer operations.

## Business Goals

- Help employees find authoritative policy information faster.
- Improve consistency and explainability without weakening human accountability.
- Reduce avoidable operational effort while preserving regulatory and contractual controls.
- Build reusable capabilities only when reuse creates measurable value.

## Users

- Service agents and operations specialists
- Claims and underwriting professionals
- Legal, compliance, privacy, and security reviewers
- Product and engineering teams
- Platform operators and business leaders

## Data Domains

Relevant domains include policies and products, customers and identities, claims, providers, reference data, documents and communications, operational events, and audit evidence. Ownership and authoritative sources must be confirmed as decisions are made.

## Risk Profile

HealthSure handles sensitive and regulated information. Incorrect, stale, unauthorized, or unexplained outputs can create customer harm, compliance exposure, financial loss, and operational incidents. Controls must be proportional to each use case rather than assumed universal.

## Program Assumptions

- The initial assistant is internal and advisory; it does not make binding coverage or claims decisions.
- Authoritative policy language and structured systems remain the systems of record.
- Human users remain accountable for consequential actions unless a later decision explicitly changes that boundary.
- Scale, latency, availability, and regional requirements are not invented before evidence exists.
- Recommendations will be revisited when usage, regulation, risk, cost, or organizational capability changes.
