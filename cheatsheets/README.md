# Cheat Sheets

Cheat sheets are five-minute reviews of an architectural topic or decision. They preserve the business question, simplest viable option, key alternatives, dominant trade-offs and cost drivers, operational risks, strongest counterargument, and recommendation-change triggers.

Name files with the related week or ADR and a lowercase descriptive slug, such as `week01-retrieval-decision.md` or `ADR-0001-retrieval-strategy.md`.

A reader should be able to understand and challenge the decision in five minutes without treating the summary as a substitute for its lesson or ADR.
