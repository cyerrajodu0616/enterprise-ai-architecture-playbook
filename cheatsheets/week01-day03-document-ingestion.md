# Five-Minute Cheat Sheet — Trustworthy Policy Ingestion

## One-sentence definition

Trustworthy ingestion preserves business meaning and proves why an exact, complete, authoritative, applicable, authorized representation was eligible for a request.

## Business problem

Extracted text can be wrong evidence when its version, package, structure, authority, applicability, or authorization is uncertain.

## Simplicity rule

Use direct authoritative historical access when it guarantees immutable retrieval and integrity; retain a governed copy only when those guarantees are insufficient.

## Eligibility ladder

`PARSED → TECHNICALLY_VALIDATED → BUSINESS_APPROVED → APPLICABLE + AUTHORIZED → ELIGIBLE`

## When to use the proposed boundary

- versioned governing documents and amendments;
- meaning-bearing tables, footnotes, images, or package relationships;
- retroactive corrections and historical replay;
- answer-to-evidence and resolver impact analysis.

## When not to overbuild

- the authoritative source already satisfies immutable historical resolution, integrity, completeness, authorization, and required service levels;
- the artifact cannot influence a material answer;
- added automation lacks real change taxonomy and validation evidence.

## Key trade-offs

- reproducibility versus sensitive duplication;
- fail-closed correctness versus availability;
- structure preservation versus parser operations;
- activation speed versus accountable approval.

## Common failure modes

- parsed text loses table or footnote meaning;
- a component validates but the package is incomplete;
- parser upgrades inherit stale validation;
- old packages remain active after applicability ends;
- content lineage misses answers that should have used a corrected amendment.

## Cost and operations

Integration, storage, parsing, validation, approval, reprocessing, lineage, security, review, replay, remediation, and incidents. Operate parser changes as governed migrations.

## Strongest counterargument

A capable authoritative source may satisfy the same boundary without a new registry or retained originals.

## Interview framing

> I separate extraction from authority and eligibility. I version every evidence-bearing dependency, activate complete applicable packages atomically, and preserve both what was used and what should have been used.

## What changes the recommendation

Source guarantees, policy semantics, actual formats, freshness, authorization, retention, scale, review capacity, defects, and measured automation evidence.
